Reports a Boolean formula that has a simpler form with the same meaning:
  • a double negation, not not p, which is p;
  • a negated disjunction or conjunction of negations: not (not a or not b) is a and b, and not (not a and not b) is a or b;
  • a negated implication: not (a implies b) is a and not b;
  • a negated comparison: not (x = y) is x != y, not (x in y) is x not in y, and not (i < j) is i >= j;
  • adjacent converse implications, a implies b and b implies a, which are a iff b.

The quick-fix rewrites the formula, keeping the text of its operands and the style of its operators (!, && and ||, or not, and and or).
