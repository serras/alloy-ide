Reports a quantified formula that has a simpler form:
  • a negated quantifier: not (some x: A | p) is no x: A | p, not (no x: A | p) is some x: A | p, and not (all x: A | not p) is some x: A | p;
  • all x: A | not p, which is no x: A | p;
  • a quantified membership, when x is the only variable and does not occur in B: all x: A | x in B is A in B, some x: A | x in B is some A & B, and no x: A | x in B is no A & B;
  • distinct variables tested by hand: all x, y: A | x != y implies p is all disj x, y: A | p, and some x, y: A | x != y and p is some disj x, y: A | p.
